# Requirements Baseline

Tattoo Platform v2 preserves the 24-system master specification covering generation, cover-ups, production preparation, protected previews, licensing, customer/artist experiences, website automation, and marketing.

## Mandatory production contract

A production-capable design must originate from one canonical locked design; produce FTA, ACR, and TRS derivatives; preserve materially identical geometry and composition; fail closed on material drift; keep ACR visibly hand-drawn; keep clean production outputs private; and require artist review before tattoo execution.

## Cover-up requirements

Original evidence is immutable and restricted. AI analysis is advisory. Physical stencil test-fit and artist disposition remain required before execution.
